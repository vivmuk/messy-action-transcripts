0Set itSep 5

The Starting Line

Pick your "delusional goal" — the version you actually want, not the safest one to admit. Exciting + a little psycho.

Exercises
  • Write down your delusional goal — the real one, not the safe one.
  • Rate your belief in it 1–10 right now (you re-rate on Day 10).
  • Begin the ritual: morning self-hypnosis before phone or email, daily through the whole challenge.
1Set itSep 6

Clarity

Get radically clear on what you actually want. Clarity is the first act of belief.

Exercises
  • 20-minute journaling date, phone in another room: If I couldn't fail, what would I want? Where is curiosity pulling me? What made my ears perk up before I shut it down? What do I already know I want?
  • Morning self-hypnosis before phone/email, then write down what came up.
  • Share one honest answer in the group chat.
2Set itSep 8

C + B + A

Clarity + Belief + Aligned messy action. Catch the gremlins in the act — then take one small nudge this week.

Exercises
  • Clarity: name what you actually want (or journal what you don't want, to rule doors out).
  • Belief: name your biggest blocks — catch the gremlins in the act.
  • Aligned messy action: pick ONE small nudge and do it this week.
  • Post "Day 2 ✅" plus your nudge in Wins & Messy Action.
3Believe itSep 9

Proof of Possibility

Build belief by proving possibility, protect your attention, and take one messy action from that belief.

Exercises
  • Decision journaling: write your objections ("I'm not ready because…", "I need to figure out…", "This isn't a good time because…") and sort fear vs. genuine blockers.
  • Proof-of-Possibility File: list at least 10 pieces of evidence your goal is possible (examples, your past wins, skills you can learn).
  • Set at least one attention boundary: mute/unfollow, app blocker, no-scroll morning.
  • Bonus: build a 2–5 song "Out-Dance Your Overthinking" playlist.
  • Take ONE messy action today.
4Believe itSep 10

Rewrite the Story

Rewrite the limiting stories you've used against yourself. "No matter what happens, I got me."

Exercises
  • "I remember when…" → what you understand now. Ex: "I remember when I thought I was inconsistent. Now I understand consistency is a skill I can practice."
  • "I forgive myself for…" — write as many as you need.
  • Release it: safely burn the pages, or another meaningful ritual to put those stories to rest.
  • Give yourself a gift: "What would bring me joy today?" — then actually do it.
  • Messy action from self-trust.
5Believe itSep 11

Fear Expose

Make friends with your fear of not getting the goal. Mantra: "High intention. Low attachment."

Exercises
  • Name every fear. For each: "What would I let that mean about me?" Then make peace with the worst case.
  • Journal: "If this happened, why would I still be okay? What would I do? How could I make the best of it?"
  • Build a safety plan — you can handle more than you think.
  • Internal why: "If I fully show up for this goal, who do I become?"
  • Own your external desires — no need to turn every desire into a saintly mission.
  • Listen: Hypnosis Audio 3 + Embodiment Audio.
6Clear itSep 12

The Glass Door

Some parts of you quietly fight the goal — not sabotage, but protection. Find the hidden agendas.

Exercises
  • Write your goal at the top of a fresh page, finish: "If I actually got this, the bad thing that might happen is…"
  • Gremlin protection audit: next to each fear, write "This gremlin is protecting me from…" (being judged, being seen, failing publicly, more responsibility…).
  • Turn each hidden agenda into an "I remember when…" and put the old program in the past.
  • Do a hypnosis track today.
  • Reflect: what have you done since joining that last week's you would have avoided? What shifts have you noticed in six days?
7Clear itSep 13

A Burnt Out Brain Cannot Think Delusionally

Discomfort and lack of capacity are not the same thing. Sometimes you need rest; sometimes you're scared and need to do it anyway.

Exercises
  • Capacity delusion audit: "What is currently eating the space my goal needs?" → "Is this actually draining my capacity, or simply uncomfortable?"
  • Choose ONE boundary this week (sleep, screen time, saying no, asking for help, pausing something, delegating) and put it in place.
  • Decide what you're protecting: "What am I willing to protect for this goal, even when it's inconvenient?"
  • Resentment audit: where are you resentful — and where do you have more power to change it than you've admitted?
  • Do your brainwashing (hypnosis) + Messy Action.
8Move through itSep 14

Delegate the How. Keep the Reps.

You don't need the whole plan — enough clarity for the next step, then let the next step reveal itself.

Exercises
  • 15 scary minutes of Messy Action: pick the smallest scary rep you've been circling and DO it today. Not researching. Not planning. The actual rep.
  • Delegate the how: write down anything you're stuck on before bed and hand it over — the universe, or your subconscious.
  • Share your rep: post "Day 8 + what your 15-minute rep was." You don't know which rep is going to open the door. Keep giving yourself at-bats.
9Move through itSep 15

The Flop, the Dip + Borrowed Belief

A flop is not a prophecy — it's data. Celebrate the rep, not just the result.

Exercises
  • Build your Evidence Log: every piece of evidence from the last 9 days that your goal is possible. Internal evidence counts — scary actions taken, beliefs shifted, faster recoveries.
  • Create a Flop Protocol: "WHEN SOMETHING FLOPS, I WILL…" — decide your response now, before your gremlin decides for you.
  • Borrowed belief: when your own belief dips, borrow someone else's. Ask whose belief you can borrow.
  • Reflect: "Where do I usually stop?" / "What do I usually make a flop mean about me?" / "After Day 10, this goal needs __ from me."
  • Celebrate a flop: post "Day 9 ✅" + something you did that did NOT immediately work.
10Keep itSep 16

The Closing Day

A different brain doesn't mean a brain that never doubts — a different relationship with doubt. Don't mistake a wobble for moving backwards.

Exercises
  • Rate your belief 1–10 again BEFORE looking at your Day 0 number, then compare.
  • Note shifts: actions, thoughts, fear, self-trust, flop recovery, what you now believe is possible.
  • Revisit the goal: if it still feels expansive, exciting + a little psycho, keep it. If you could go bigger — go bigger. Write: "MY DELUSIONAL GOAL IS: ___"
  • Finish yesterday's rep: "After Day 10, this goal needs ___ from me" — then do ONE thing today that honors it.
  • Keep brainwashing: no Day 11. Hypnosis before phone, lifetime access, journaling, Messy Action as continual practice.
  • Remember: you don't need 100% belief — 5% "yeah… but maybe?" is enough to work with.
