# The Brainwash Challenge — archive

The 10-day Brainwash Challenge by Grace Brodeur (Sep 5–16, 2026): an 11-day arc
(Day 0 ritual through Day 10 closing) on installing a delusional belief and taking
messy action on it daily.

- The interactive 11-day summary lives at [challenge.html](../challenge.html) on the site.
- [`transcripts/`](transcripts/) — full machine transcripts of the 10 teaching audios
  (Days 1–10; Day 0 has no teaching audio). Transcribed from the Kajabi episode MP3s
  with Venice (`openai/whisper-large-v3`). Machine-generated; minor errors possible.
- [`summaries/`](summaries/) — per-day summaries distilled from the transcripts:
  core teaching, exercises & assignments, key verbatim quotes, and notes.
