# Day 8 — The How (Sep 14)

## Core teaching
- You don't need the full plan or the complete route — your job today is just the next step. "You don't need the entire map. You need enough clarity to identify the next step."
- Delegate the "how" — spiritually or to your subconscious. Write the problem down before bed, ask for help, then keep moving where you do have clarity.
- Skills are figure-out-able; lack of skills today is not the problem. The problem is refusing to start until you have both skills and the full plan. "80% of this is going to be managing my mind… the other 20% is going to be me learning some skills."
- Think of progress as "at-bats" — every swing teaches you something, and even the no's are reps. The more swings you take, the more chances to hit something.
- Locking in matters: most people who built something allowing them to work few hours had a period where they were really locked in. "I'm not going to be the person who tells you you can create your delusional goal without lifting a finger."

## Exercises & assignments
- **15 scary minutes**: "15 minutes of the thing you've been avoiding, whether that's the email, the first page, the application, the pitch, the post, whatever your next little step is, that you know would probably be really helpful to take but you've secretly been putting off a little bit." Your only agreement is 15 minutes; if it turns into an hour, great, but don't go in expecting that.
- **Delegate a question to the universe**: "identify a question that you're feeling a little bit foggy on that you would like to delegate to the universe." Language doesn't matter — God, spirit, subconscious — identify what you're foggy on and set the intention for the answer to come back.
- **Post your rep in the group**: "come into the group and post what your 15 minute rep was." Even if it feels tiny, post it anyway.
- **Show someone else some love** in the group while you're in there.

## Key quotes
- "The full plan. The exact route from where you are right now to where you are going. And that part is actually not your job today."
- "You have to be willing to eat shit longer than other people around you who give up."
- "Sometimes you don't need a new strategy. You just need someone to say, hey, stay put and keep showing up."
- "I am the person who can do this just as much as anybody else who wants to."
