# Day 4 — The Brainwash Challenge (Sep 10)

## Core teaching
- Different "gremlins" (self-sabotaging thoughts) come in different flavors — telling you it's not possible, not worth it, you're not worthy, or you won't follow through — and each needs to be handled individually.
- Procrastination isn't laziness or lack of capability: "You don't avoid actions. You avoid feelings." We avoid the fear of failure, judgment, embarrassment, or discovering we're not good enough.
- Action cures fear faster than mindset work. Brainwashing helps, but at some point you must feel the fear and act anyway — the experience itself teaches your brain it's survivable.
- Self-love and self-trust are your best defenses when going after big goals. Judgment has far less power when it doesn't confirm something cruel you've already said to yourself.
- You are your own first relationship. Building internal safety — "no matter what, I got me" — makes scary goals doable.

## Exercises & assignments
- **Forgiveness / reframing exercise:** Pull out your journal and start with "I remember when" — list the stories and patterns you've been using as evidence against yourself, and follow each one with "Now I understand…" (She provides many examples, including: "I remember when I used to not follow through and that it was because I wasn't capable or disciplined enough. Now I understand that I was overwhelmed, scared, and trying to protect myself from feelings I didn't know how to handle yet.")
- **Self-forgiveness exercise:** Start each line with "I forgive myself for…" — get as specific as you can with your own inventory of things you've been holding over your head. "Anything you are still holding over your head gets to come out here."
- **Burn or release the papers:** Burn the papers safely (fireproof surface, water nearby) and let yourself release the stories. If you don't want to burn them, shred or keep them — light a candle and set the intention of putting the stories to rest.
- **Gift yourself a treat:** Ask yourself "What would bring me joy today?" and give yourself that — flowers, a latte, a bath, sitting in the sun, time in the park. "Give yourself the gift."

## Key quotes
- "You don't avoid actions. You avoid feelings."
- "Action cures fear. Stop waiting to fully get over it. Taking the action cures the fear."
- "It is your birthright to go after what you want. There is no need to shrink your desire."
- "You are your own first relationship, and you must prioritize yourself as such."
