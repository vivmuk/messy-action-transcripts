# Day 5 — How to Not Get Attached to the Timeline (Sep 11)

## Core teaching
- The sweet spot is wanting something deeply without needing it to prove your worth: "I really, really want this. I am willing to work for it. I believe I can create it and I will still be okay if life unfolds differently than I currently expect."
- The stickiest fear isn't the external outcome—it's what you would let that outcome *mean about you* (e.g., that you're unlovable, a failure, not good enough).
- These fears live "rent-free in your brain and call the shots" subconsciously, so you must expose them and treat them as data, not truths.
- When your worth is tangled up in the goal, every setback feels meaningful and makes it harder to stay in the process.
- A powerful internal "why" is who you become in the process of pursuing the goal—skills, confidence, courage, resilience—because the timeline can't take those away from you.
- It's okay to own selfish desires (like wanting a dream home or money for options), and it's also okay to have external whys (like serving your community); just don't make any single outcome a requirement for a beautiful life.

## Exercises & assignments
- Ask yourself: "What am I afraid will happen if I don't get this? And even more importantly, what would I let that mean about me?"
- "Write down every fear you have about not getting your goal and what you would let each one mean about you." Expose as many as possible—"The winner is the one who exposes the most."
- Face the worst case and journal: "If this actually happened, why would I be able to handle it? What would I do? How could I make the best of it? Why would this not be the end all of my life?"
- Build a concrete plan around your worst case (e.g., emergency fund, reaching out to network, changing business model) to make action feel safer and reduce procrastination.
- Reframe how you'll talk to yourself if it doesn't work out—commit to championing yourself regardless of the result.
- Journal on your internal why: "If you show up fully for this goal for the next 100 days, who do you become in the process? Why is becoming that person important to you? What skills will you build? How will it change your confidence? How will it change the relationship you have with yourself?"
- Journal on your external whys: "How could achieving this goal impact the people who matter to you, including yourself? What experiences could it create? What could it allow you to give? What could it allow you to have?"
- Own your desires without shame; notice any "charge" or gremlin that says it's unsafe to want nice things, and set it aside.
- "Take some time to make peace with releasing the timeline." Adopt the mantra: "high intention, low attachment."

## Key quotes
- "You do not have to desperately need something in order to wholeheartedly pursue it."
- "Meanwhile, the thought is already living rent-free in your brain and calling the shots, okay? So what we need to do is fear expose."
- "You do not have to turn every desire into some saintly mission to justify wanting it."
- "Fall in love with who you become while doing it, and then release the death grip on exactly when and how it has to happen."
