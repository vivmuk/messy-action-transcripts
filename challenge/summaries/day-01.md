# Day 1 — The Clarity Work (Sep 6)

## Core teaching
- Most people don't have a clarity problem — they have a "fuzziness problem." The knowing is usually there, it's just blurry because every time it surfaces, a fear shoves it back under before you can look at it directly.
- Grace's realization: "I wasn't stuck. I didn't lack clarity. I was lacking the audacity." She wasn't admitting what she wanted because she didn't think she could achieve it.
- The belief to install today: "If a desire is on your heart, it is because it is meant to be. And it can be." The desire comes first — it is the compass, not something you need to qualify for with a full plan or credentials.
- Curiosity is a compass. The little exchange where you think "maybe I could do that" and your brain immediately goes "no, no, no, don't be silly" happens in half a second — and in that half second, entire dreams get filed away.
- Clarity is a product of two things: time with self (TWS) and micro action on the little bits of clarity you already have. Today is the TWS part; tomorrow covers micro action.
- Her mentor's framing: once you find "the centerpiece of your mosaic," all the open questions become yes or no answers — does this get me closer to that centerpiece or not?

## Exercises & assignments
- **TWS date (20 minutes):** Put your phone in another room, get your journal (the one where everything you write becomes true), and write without editing yourself. Answer these questions in order:
  - "If I knew I could not fail, what would I want?"
  - "Where has my curiosity been pulling me that I keep dismissing?"
  - "What did my ears perk up on at some point in my life that I shut down with 'I can never do that.' 'That's silly.' 'Be practical.'?"
  - "What do I already know I want to do that I've been pretending not to know?"
- After those questions, look at the delusional goal from day zero and ask: "Is this still the one?" You're allowed to refine or completely change it — several days remain to lock in the final version.
- **Do your first hypnosis session** (okay if not in the morning this time).
- **Come into the group chat and share one honest answer** from the journaling, even if it's just something you've been pretending not to want. Grace is sharing hers too.

## Notes
- **Self-hypnosis guidance (audio track):** Recommended first thing in the morning, before email/work/phone, because coming out of sleep your brain is in a slower, dreamier, cleaner-slate state. Splash water on your face first so you don't fall asleep, but don't overstimulate. If you have kids or a busy household, try waking 15 minutes earlier. Midday is acceptable if that's all you have; listening as you fall asleep at night also works. Safeguard the morning time — gremlins multiply during the day and talk you out of taking action.
- **What a light trance feels like:** Very relaxed, body gets heavier, thoughts slow down, a drifty feeling like right before sleep. You will not feel "hypnotized" or "gone." If your brain says "I just feel relaxed, I don't think it worked" — that is the start. The change builds quietly, rep after rep; around day 10 you may wake up and feel your brain is slightly different.
- **For people who don't see mental pictures:** You don't need pictures. Focus on the feeling — sense what it would be like, know that it is there. "The feeling is what your brain actually logs."
- **You are in control the entire time.** This is not a state someone puts you in. In the first track she has you make your own legs heavy so you can notice they actually obey you. You can open your eyes whenever you want.
- **Disclaimer:** This is not a replacement for therapy.
- **Consistency setup:** If you've struggled with consistency, ask yourself "how can I make this as easy as possible?" — get an accountability partner, do it together, identify a treat for showing up all 10 days. Set this up now while motivation is high.
- **For anyone feeling totally foggy** about career direction or the goal itself: start with something high-level you definitely know you want more of (work for yourself remotely, more time freedom, location freedom, financial freedom) and build belief on that. A brain that believes starts solution-brainstorming mode.
