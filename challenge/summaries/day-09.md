# Day 9 — What Happens When the Brainwashing Starts Wearing Off (Sep 15)

## Core teaching
- There's a particular moment in every new goal/habit/project where the novelty wears off and you land in "the dip" — you've taken reps but don't have evidence yet, and a gremlin says "see? this is why we don't do this." Most people quit here.
- The emotional cycle of change starts with uninformed optimism (this is going to be amazing!) then informed pessimism (this is harder than I thought). Nobody romanticizes the middle — the part where you're doing the reps and don't have enough evidence yet.
- Follow through is not a personality trait. When someone repeatedly stops, Grace looks at two things: either the way they're doing it is unsustainable, or they don't have enough belief that the effort will be worth it (a third case being anxiety/overwhelm).
- Belief operates in a loop: belief creates the rep → the rep creates evidence → the evidence creates more belief. It's not one-and-done.
- Losing belief is sneaky — "you don't dramatically quit the dream. You quietly settle." You stop doing the volume, stop experimenting, stop putting the same energy behind growth.
- Borrowed belief is a legitimate tool: coaches, community, people who can point out blind spots or stretch your ceiling. But it's a bridge — eventually it has to become your own evidence through reps.
- Flops (flopped videos, ignored pitches, rejected applications) are data, not prophecies. "They are data. That's it."

## Exercises & assignments
- **Create an evidence log.** "Go through the last nine days and write down every piece of evidence that supports the idea that your goal is possible." Internal evidence counts — actions you avoided but took, belief going from a 3 to a 4 or 5, an idea, clarity, a mindset shift.
- **Celebrate yourself with a small treat** — favorite latte, a walk to the park, something that feels like a reward.
- **Create your flop protocol.** "Literally write down when something flops, I will..." and decide it now, before you're emotional. (e.g., look at the data, ask what you learned, celebrate taking the rep, adjust, take the next step.)
- **Answer these four questions:**
  - "Where do I usually stop?"
  - "What do I usually make a flop mean about me?"
  - "Whose belief can I borrow when mine dips?"
  - "What structure makes it easier for me to keep going?"
- **Finish the sentence:** "After day 10 this goal needs ___" — write the first thing that comes up (consistency, courage, more reps, structure, patience, etc.).
- **Post in the group:** "day 9 + celebrate a flop" — share something that didn't work, in the last nine days or in the past (a rejection, failed launch, video nobody watched).

## Key quotes
- "Nobody romanticizes the middle."
- "You don't dramatically quit the dream. You quietly settle."
- "Borrowed belief is a bridge. Eventually, we want to turn it into your own evidence. And the only way we can do that is through enough reps."
- "You either get the lesson you needed or the outcome you wanted. There is no failure. There is only feedback."
