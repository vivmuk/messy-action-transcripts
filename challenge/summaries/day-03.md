# Day 3 — (no explicit title given; theme: belief & possibility) (Sep 9)

## Core teaching
- You can reinvent yourself — your work, money, relationships, confidence, environment — at any point you decide to participate in changing it. There is no universal timeline; you're not "behind" because that would require one shared schedule everyone was supposed to follow.
- Grace identifies five things her periods of rapid change had in common: (1) making a decision, (2) collecting proof of possibility, (3) outdancing overthinking, (4) brainwashing consistently, and (5) putting up blinders while taking messy action daily.
- Belief is something you practice through repetition — feeling great one morning doesn't mean you'll never doubt again, so you keep exposing your brain to the new story.
- Visualization isn't a replacement for action; it's rehearsal for it. Mentally practicing the future-you state makes that future less foreign when you start behaving like the person who creates it.
- You don't need to already be extraordinary; you need "the audacity to actually use it."

## Exercises & assignments
- **Proof of possibility file:** "Why is your delusional goal possible? Who has done something similar? Who has done it with a background similar to yours? Who started later? who started with fewer resources. What have you already done that proves you can learn difficult things?"
- **Catch the "behind" thought:** When your brain says "I should have done this three years ago," redirect it: "Cool, you didn't. We are here now. And what are we going to do with it today?"
- **Decision check-in:** "Have you actually decided that you are full sending this goal. Or are you still negotiating with it?"
- **Build a playlist** for when overthinking spirals hit — dance/move physically to interrupt the mental loop. She links one in the show notes as a starting point.
- **Quiet the outside noise:** go for walks listening to nothing, protect your morning peace, block distracting apps (she mentions the app Jomo), stop spending time with people who consistently drain you.
- **Follow intuitive nudges** — act on the small day-to-day ones to build trust for the bigger ones.
- **Do today's journaling prompts** and **listen to the new brainwashing audio** (visualization-based, feeling into the version of you who already has the goal).

## Key quotes
- "You are not behind because behind requires there to be one universal timeline. that everyone was supposed to be following in the first place. and there isn't."
- "If it's possible for her, it's possible for me."
- "The answers you seek are in the silence you're avoiding."
- "If you show up every day the results won't have a choice."
